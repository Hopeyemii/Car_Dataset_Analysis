# Car_Dataset_Analysis

## Project Overview
The Car Sales Analysis provides an overview of sales performance and customer vehicle preferences. The project analyses key performance indicators (KPIs), compares sales across customer income groups, and examines preferences for car models, brands, body styles, and transmission types.
The goal is to transform car sales data into meaningful insights that support business decision-making, customer segmentation, and inventory planning.

## Project Objectives
- Evaluate overall sales performance using key performance indicators.
- Compare revenue, cars sold, and growth rates across customer income groups.
- Identify preferred car models and brands among different income groups.
- Analyse customer preferences for vehicle body styles and transmission types.
- Provide data-driven recommendations for sales and inventory planning.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Revenue: $671,525,465
Total Cars Sold: 23,906
Average Selling Value: $28,090
Growth Rate: 24%

## Key Insights
1. Sales Performance by Customer Income Group
- Middle-income customers contributed the highest revenue and sales volume.
- Low-income customers recorded the highest growth rate at 34%.
- High-income customers contributed the lowest revenue and sales volume.

2. Customer Vehicle Preferences
Car Models
- LS400 was the leading displayed preference among low-income customers at 23.69% and middle-income customers at 23.98%.
- Ram Pickup led among high-income customers at 22.25%.

Car Brands
- Chevrolet led among low-income customers at 23.37% and middle-income customers at 23.92%.
- Dodge led among high-income customers at 22.63%.

Body Style
- SUVs were the leading preferred body style across all three income groups.

Transmission Type
- Automatic transmission accounted for 52.59% of displayed preferences.
- Manual transmission accounted for 47.41%.

## Business Recommendations
1. Investigate the factors contributing to the low-income group's 34% growth rate.
2. Use customer preferences to inform inventory planning and vehicle selection.
3. Review the high-income group's sales performance to identify potential opportunities for improvement.
4. Monitor revenue, sales volume, average selling value, and growth rate regularly.
5. Use customer segmentation insights to support targeted marketing strategies.
